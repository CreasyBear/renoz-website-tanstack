# RENOZ Campaign Planning

Create a focused campaign in which the buyer, business outcome, evidence, channel role, economics, assets, measurement, and approvals agree with one another.

Unless the user specifies another outcome, plan toward a predictable intake of qualified off-grid sales leads from serviceable locations in Western Australia. Do not optimise for publicity, traffic, clicks, or raw form submissions at the expense of sales acceptance.

## Ground every claim

Use `PRODUCT.md`, `src/data/product-catalog.ts`, and relevant RENOZ guides, insights, case studies, documents, and routes as the source of truth. Use first-party campaign and CRM exports for performance claims. Mark material statements as confirmed, inferred, or missing evidence. Unsupported commercial claims become constraints, not copy.

## Choose the mode

- **Plan:** design a campaign from a business outcome and budget.
- **Media decision:** compare paid, owned, earned, partner, event, PR, or content options.
- **Brief:** turn an approved strategy into a production and launch brief.
- **Audit or postmortem:** reconcile outcomes, explain limits, and capture the next decision.

Read [campaign-brief.md](references/campaign-brief.md) for the working template. Read [channels-and-measurement.md](references/channels-and-measurement.md) when selecting channels, assigning budget, or evaluating results. If Meta is selected, use the companion `$renoz-meta-ads` skill for account readiness and test design.

## Method

1. State one primary business outcome and the decision the campaign must enable.
2. Define one buyer, buying job, geography, serviceability rule, and exclusions. Split distinct buyers rather than averaging them into a vague market.
3. Select a supportable offer and evidence hierarchy. Do not begin with a channel or content format.
4. Map the buyer journey, then assign each channel one role: create demand, capture intent, build trust, convert, nurture, or re-engage.
5. Check current channel availability, rate cards, targeting, policy, and measurement limits using official or first-party evidence. Date mutable facts.
6. Concentrate a constrained budget on the fewest channels and test cells that can produce a decision. Separate fixed sponsorship or production costs from working media.
7. Inventory the proof and assets needed, including permissions, claims review, destinations, forms, sales handoff, and CRM fields.
8. Define the deepest reliable success metric and how platform, analytics, enquiry, and CRM records will reconcile.
9. Register budget, hypothesis, control or comparison, observation window, pause condition, scale condition, owner, and review date.
10. After the campaign, distinguish observed results from inference, reconcile spend to sales outcomes, and record what to retain, retest, stop, or investigate.

## Deliverable

Return:

- decision summary and primary outcome;
- confirmed facts, assumptions, missing inputs, and claim constraints;
- buyer, buying job, geography, qualification, and exclusions;
- offer, message hierarchy, proof, and CTA;
- buyer journey and channel roles;
- campaign architecture, destinations, sales handoff, and owners;
- asset and production plan;
- budget scenarios, working media, fixed costs, reserve, and maximum loss;
- measurement map and attribution limitations;
- registered experiment and operating rules;
- risks, permissions, and approval gates; and
- post-campaign scorecard or review plan.

Never invent market size, prices, media rates, reach, benchmarks, competitor spend, conversion rates, revenue, or profitability. Research current official rate cards or obtain quotes when the decision requires them. Otherwise provide the formula and missing inputs.

## Authorization boundary

Research, comparison, planning, drafts, and audits are allowed. Do not purchase media, book sponsorships, issue a release, submit to a newswire, publish content, upload audiences, change budgets, launch campaigns, or contact publishers, partners, journalists, or customers without explicit authorization. Before an authorized mutation, restate the supplier or account, deliverable, audience, timing, total cost, renewal terms, and cancellation or rollback path.

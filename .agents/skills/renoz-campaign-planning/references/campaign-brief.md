# Campaign brief

Use only the sections required for the decision, but do not omit evidence, economics, measurement, or approval gates.

For the default RENOZ objective, define the outcome as sales-accepted off-grid leads in Western Australia and name the criteria that distinguish them from general enquiries.

## 2. Evidence ledger

| Statement or input | Status | Source and date | Campaign implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product fact | Confirmed / inferred / missing | RENOZ source | Allowed claim or constraint |
| Buyer problem | Confirmed / inferred / missing | Interview, CRM, search, or sales evidence | Message or research need |
| Channel fact | Confirmed / inferred / missing | Official source, quote, or export | Budget or feasibility |
| Economics | Confirmed / scenario / missing | Finance or sales owner | Acquisition ceiling |

Use exact model and scope when stating technical facts or certifications. Do not generalise one product's evidence across the range.

## 3. Audience and buying job

Define:

- buyer or account type;
- trigger and job they need completed;
- location and serviceability;
- off-grid, fringe-of-grid, or grid-connected status;
- project size or application where known;
- decision participants and likely objections;
- explicit exclusions; and
- what makes a lead sales-accepted.

## 4. Offer

State the useful exchange, not merely the CTA. Examples may include a project-fit conversation, technical discovery, sizing input checklist, regional guide, case study, or site-assessment pathway. Confirm operational capacity to fulfil it.

## 5. Message and proof hierarchy

1. Buyer problem or decision.
2. RENOZ approach in plain language.
3. Product, process, local, or case-study proof.
4. Limits, suitability conditions, or required assessment.
5. Specific next step and response expectation.

Avoid unsupported savings, universal ROI, absolute reliability, unqualified Australian-made claims, artificial scarcity, rebate certainty, or comparisons without an equivalent basis.

Give every paid placement, article, event, email, guide, or partner activation a specific role. Remove channels that have no necessary role.

## 7. Campaign architecture

For each campaign unit specify buyer, geography, channel, audience, offer, creative, destination, conversion, owner, start and end, and exclusions. Separate materially different buyers or conversions.

## 8. Asset plan

| Asset | Purpose | Evidence needed | Source or generation route | Formats | Owner | Approval |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Prefer authentic RENOZ and permissioned project assets. Public or generated imagery may support concepts, but must not imply a real RENOZ installation, partnership, product configuration, manufacturing process, or customer outcome unless that fact is true and approved.

Record currency, tax treatment, minimum commitments, cancellation terms, billing timing, and whether costs recur. Keep fixed sponsorship and distribution fees visible; do not hide them inside working media.

Define the primary business metric, diagnostic metrics, event and CRM mapping, UTM convention, attribution setting, reconciliation cadence, sales disposition rules, and known blind spots.

## 12. Risks and approvals

List claim, legal, privacy, brand, customer permission, platform policy, inventory, fulfilment, sales-capacity, supplier, and measurement risks. Assign an owner and deadline to every launch blocker.

## 13. Postmortem

Record spend, delivery, valid leads, qualified leads, opportunities, wins, sales lag, attribution gaps, significant changes, observed findings, inferences, inconclusive questions, and the next budget decision.

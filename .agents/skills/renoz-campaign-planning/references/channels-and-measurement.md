# Channel selection and measurement

## Channel hypotheses for RENOZ

These are starting hypotheses, not recommendations or performance promises.

| Channel | Likely role | Main limitation | Evidence needed before or during spend |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Search | Capture explicit off-grid, rural battery, generator, and regional intent | Demand and keyword economics may be limited | Current search terms, geography, qualified leads, costs, and impression share |
| Meta | Create and retarget consideration among WA property owners and rural operators | Lower intent and creative-dependent lead quality | Qualified rate, creative learning, CRM outcomes, and placement data |
| LinkedIn | Reach trade, engineering, commercial, and project roles | High cost and small relevant audiences | Account or role match, qualified conversations, and opportunities |
| Trade or regional media | Reach a concentrated farming, property, energy, or industry audience | Paid placement does not automatically create ranking authority | Audience evidence, referral visits, enquiries, branded search, and any earned coverage |
| PR or earned media | Build credibility around a genuine milestone, project, partnership, or useful data | Coverage and links are not guaranteed | Relevant editorial coverage, referral activity, citations, and earned links |
| Partner marketing | Borrow trust and distribution through installer, supplier, association, or project ecosystems | Requires aligned incentives, permissions, and lead ownership | Partner referrals, co-generated leads, meetings, and opportunities |
| Events | Build relationships and technical credibility | Fixed cost, staff load, and delayed attribution | Scanned qualified contacts, booked meetings, opportunities, and follow-up completion |
| Email | Nurture known, consented contacts and reactivate appropriate leads | Depends on consent, list quality, and useful segmentation | Replies, high-intent actions, qualified leads, and opportunities |
| Owned content | Answer buyer questions and support search, sales, ads, and PR | Publishing alone does not create distribution | Search demand, assisted conversions, citations, backlinks, and sales use |

For off-grid WA lead intake, start by testing intent capture and one demand-creation route before buying broad awareness. A newswire or sponsored article is justified only when it reaches the buyer or creates a credible earned-media opportunity; distribution counts alone are not a business outcome.

## Selection scorecard

Score each option using evidence, not enthusiasm:

- match to buyer and serviceable geography;
- role in the buying journey;
- evidence and assets available;
- current rate, minimum commitment, and opportunity cost;
- ability to measure qualified sales outcomes;
- operational and sales follow-up load;
- speed and durability of learning;
- claim, privacy, brand, and supplier risk; and
- durable value after spend ends, such as reusable content, relationships, citations, or permissioned assets.

If the budget is constrained, select one primary paid channel and let owned content, sales follow-up, and measurement support it. Add a second paid channel only when it answers a distinct question or captures a distinct stage.

## Budget logic

Build from business economics:

```text
affordable acquisition cost = expected contribution × acceptable acquisition share
test budget = affordable acquisition cost × outcomes needed for a decision
channel allocation = test units + production + fixed media + reserve
```

Use conservative, base, and upside scenarios when contribution, close rate, or qualified-lead rate is unknown. Keep a visible reserve and separate fixed sponsorship, newswire, event, or production fees from variable media. State the maximum amount that can be lost while still producing useful learning.

## Outcome hierarchy

```text
won customer or contribution
  > CRM opportunity
  > sales-accepted or qualified lead
  > valid enquiry
  > website conversion
  > platform conversion
  > landing-page view
  > click or delivery
```

Do not compare channels on a shallow metric when a deeper common outcome exists.

## Cross-channel UTMs

Use a stable convention such as:

```text
utm_source=<publisher-or-platform>
utm_medium=<paid_search|paid_social|sponsorship|referral|email|pr>
utm_campaign=<buyer>_<objective>_<offer>_<yyyy-mm>
utm_content=<concept-or-placement>_<format>_<variant>
utm_term=<keyword-audience-or-test-cell>
```

Preserve the original source in CRM, capture subsequent touches where possible, and document how direct, phone, partner, and offline enquiries are handled.

## Review rules

- Validate links, forms, tracking, CRM routing, sales response, and reporting before judging media.
- Use early checks for delivery, spend, broken experiences, and obviously irrelevant leads—not premature winner selection.
- Evaluate marginal cost per qualified lead as spend changes.
- Change one meaningful variable at a time where practical and date all material changes.
- Do not claim incrementality without a defensible comparison, holdout, time-series design, or other causal evidence.
- Allow an inconclusive result when the evidence does not support a decision.

## Post-campaign scorecard

Report:

- planned and actual spend by production, fixed media, and working media;
- delivery and destination behaviour;
- valid, qualified, opportunity, and win counts;
- cost per valid and qualified lead where denominators are reliable;
- sales response time and disposition completeness;
- channel and CRM reconciliation gaps;
- observed evidence, inference, and missing evidence;
- reusable assets, relationships, citations, or earned links; and
- the next action: stop, retain, retest, scale, or investigate.

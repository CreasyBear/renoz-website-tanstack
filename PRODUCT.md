# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

English-speaking battery industry readers who cannot readily access or interpret Chinese battery-industry sources distributed through WeChat: developers, EPCs, integrators, procurement, manufacturers, suppliers, investors, advisers, and commercial buyers.

## Product Purpose

RENOZ Energy supplies residential, rural, and commercial BESS in Western Australia. The China Battery Desk publishes source-grounded English briefings from inside the Chinese battery supply chain. Success is that readers treat RENOZ as an authoritative interpreter of that material, not as a republisher of scans.

## Positioning

Discover hard-to-access Chinese sources, keep one source to one job, sandwich the evidence, convert yuan for scale, and explain why it matters outside China. Editorial judgment is the product.

## Operating Context

Durable English pages from WeChat-hosted, often Chinese-language material. Preserve publisher, URL, dates, geography, units, observed-versus-forecast status, methodology gaps, and a RENOZ interpretation. WeChat timestamps often fail; do not invent them.

## Constraints

- Authority-led. No sales CTA, weekly cadence, or non-functional newsletter form.
- Observed quotations, estimates, forecasts, and RENOZ interpretations stay distinct.
- Do not republish source scans or full report translations without permission.
- Do not invent search demand, rankings, or audience size.
- No CJK on published insight surfaces.

## Brand

Perth-based, confident, concise, technically credible, evidence-led. Close to the Chinese supply chain, still recognisably RENOZ.

## Principles

1. Translate access into understanding, not copied content.
2. Put provenance, units, dates, and uncertainty beside the claim they qualify.
3. A China powder print is not a WA pack price.
4. Earn authority through specific evidence and candid limits.
5. Build a source-traced archive, not disposable news.

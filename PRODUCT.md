# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience for the RENOZ China Battery Desk is the wider English-speaking battery industry: project developers, EPCs, integrators, procurement teams, manufacturers, suppliers, investors, advisers, commercial buyers, and technically engaged readers who cannot readily access or interpret Chinese battery-industry sources distributed through WeChat.

## Product Purpose

RENOZ Energy supplies residential, rural, and commercial battery energy storage systems in Western Australia. The China Battery Desk extends the website with source-grounded English-language intelligence from inside the Chinese battery supply chain. Its immediate job is to make useful Chinese material accessible and intelligible; success is that readers recognise RENOZ as an authoritative voice on batteries, battery materials, pricing, technology, and supply-chain developments.

## Positioning

RENOZ discovers difficult-to-access Chinese battery-industry sources, selects material with real signal, translates terminology and units accurately, separates observations from forecasts, and explains why the information matters outside China. The differentiator is editorial judgment and Australian battery-industry interpretation, not wholesale republication.

## Operating Context

The desk publishes durable English web briefings from source material that may be image-based, Chinese-language, lightly indexed, or unavailable outside authenticated WeChat access. Each briefing must preserve original source, publisher, publication date, access date, geography, units, observed-versus-forecast status, methodology gaps, and an original RENOZ interpretation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Launch scope: a durable China Battery Desk landing surface plus one substantial flagship briefing.
- The first launch uses a 12 August 2026 daily lithium-battery-material price bulletin from 马里亚纳锂电 and a 12 August 2026 SDIC Securities lithium-market report.
- The publication is authority-led rather than forcing an immediate quote, partnership, or newsletter conversion.
- The current site has existing commercial and partnership contact flows but no genuine newsletter subscription workflow; the launch must not present a non-functional signup form.
- Observed quotations, estimates, forecasts, and RENOZ interpretations must remain visibly distinct.
- Source scans, complete tables, and full report translations must not be republished without established permission or licensing.
- Search demand, rankings, audience size, and commercial outcomes are unknown and must not be invented.

## Brand Commitments

RENOZ Energy is a Perth-based battery business. Public copy is confident, concise, technically credible, evidence-led, and never defensive. The desk should feel close to the Chinese battery supply chain while remaining recognisably RENOZ. The supplied brief requires a beautiful, presentable, unique, organic reading experience that avoids generic AI-generated design and needless hand-rolled infrastructure.

## Product Principles

1. Translate access into understanding, not copied content.
2. Put provenance, units, dates, and uncertainty beside the claim they qualify.
3. Give readers the commercially useful implication without pretending upstream prices map one-for-one to installed BESS prices.
4. Earn authority through specific evidence and candid limits rather than self-description.
5. Build a cumulative, source-traced intelligence archive rather than disposable news posts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The publication must remain readable and navigable with keyboard, screen reader, reduced-motion preferences, 200% zoom, and narrow mobile viewports. Chinese names and terms should be preserved where identity or translation precision matters, with clear English context.

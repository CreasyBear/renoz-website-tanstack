# RENOZ Energy — Offer & Positioning Brief

## Who RENOZ Is

RENOZ Energy Pty Ltd is a Perth-based battery OEM (Unit 4, 8 Murphy Street, O'Connor WA 6163), founded 2024. RENOZ designs and engineers its own modular low-voltage LiFePO4 battery storage systems. It is not an installer, not a reseller, and not an importer of a third-party product. No other company in the WA BESS market occupies the Perth-based OEM position.

## Core Product — LV-5KWH100AH

- **Chemistry:** LiFePO4 (lithium iron phosphate), 16S1P prismatic cells
- **Capacity:** 5.12 kWh per module, 100 Ah at 51.2 V nominal (48 V compatible)
- **Scalability:** Up to 8 modules per tower (~40.96 kWh); up to 6 towers in parallel (~245 kWh)
- **Cycle life:** 6,000 cycles at 80 % DoD; 10,000 cycles at 50 % DoD
- **Operating temperature:** −10 °C to 55 °C
- **Ingress protection:** IP65 (outdoor rated)
- **Weight / dimensions:** 53 kg; 600 × 440 × 175 mm per module
- **BMS:** Active cell balancing; CAN, RS485, WiFi comms; individual cell voltage and temperature monitoring
- **Certifications:** IEC 62619, UN 38.3, AS/NZS 5139, CE, RCM
- **Warranty:** 10-year replacement (not pro-rated); 15 years pro-rated
- **Inverter compatibility:** Victron, Selectronic, Deye, GoodWe, Sungrow, Growatt, SMA, Fronius (inverter-agnostic)

## Business Model

RENOZ is B2B-first. It sells through and with installer and integrator partners, who deliver, commission, and service systems for end customers. RENOZ does not install directly. End customers (residential, rural/agribusiness, commercial 50–200 kWh) are served via partners or inbound through renoz.energy, with CTAs pointing to `/contact`, `/products/*`, or `/resources` — not a shopping cart.

## Rebates (2026 — verify before publishing)

**Federal — Cheaper Home Batteries Program (CHBP):** ~30 % upfront discount via STCs at point of sale; no application required; not means-tested. ~$252–$272 per usable kWh. **Off-grid systems ARE eligible** (DCCEEW-confirmed). STC value steps down every 6 months from 1 Jan 2027 — 2026 installs lock a larger discount. Source: energy.gov.au / dcceew.gov.au.

**WA — WA Residential Battery Scheme (WARBS):** Synergy customers $130/usable kWh (cap $1,300); Horizon Power customers $380/kWh (cap $3,800). Requires grid connection, VPP enrolment (minimum 2 years), battery on the Supported Solutions List, and SAA-accredited installer on Plenti's vendor directory. **Fully off-grid systems do NOT qualify.** Source: wa.gov.au / plenti.com.au / synergy.net.au.

Stacked federal + WA: up to ~$5,000 (Synergy area) or ~$7,500 (Horizon area) on a 10 kWh grid-tied system.

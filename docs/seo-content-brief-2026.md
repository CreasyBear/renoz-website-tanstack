## Verified 2026 rebate facts (cite these URLs as proofLinks)

### Federal — Cheaper Home Batteries Program (CHBP) — ACTIVE
- ~30% upfront discount via STCs at point of sale; no application, not means-tested. Currently ~$252–$272 per usable kWh (≈$2,500–$2,700 off a 10 kWh battery). Runs to 31 Dec 2030; funding expanded from $2.3B to $7.2B (Dec 2025).
- Eligible battery size 5–100 kWh (full rate on first 14 kWh; from 1 May 2026 tiered: 0–14 kWh 100%, 14–28 kWh 60%, 28–50 kWh 15% STC factor). STC value steps down every 6 months from 1 Jan 2027 — installing earlier locks a bigger discount.
- **Off-grid systems ARE eligible** — no VPP or grid connection required (DCCEEW-confirmed). This is RENOZ's key message: off-grid buyers still get ~30% off the battery.
- Requirements: CEC-approved battery, SAA-accredited installer.
- Sources: https://www.energy.gov.au/rebates/cheaper-home-batteries-program ; https://www.dcceew.gov.au/energy/programs/cheaper-home-batteries

### WA State — WA Residential Battery Scheme (WARBS) — OPEN (to ~2027), grid-connected only
- Synergy customers: $130 per usable kWh, capped at $1,300 (10 kWh). Horizon Power customers: $380/kWh, capped at $3,800.
- Administered via Plenti; accredited vendor applies on the customer's behalf. Battery/inverter must be on the Synergy or Horizon **Supported Solutions List**; installer SAA-accredited and on the Plenti vendor directory.
- **Mandatory VPP enrolment** (Synergy Battery Rewards / Horizon Community Wave), minimum 2 years → **fully off-grid systems do NOT qualify for the WA scheme** (federal CHBP only).
- Optional no-interest loan via Plenti: $2,001–$10,000, 3–10 yr terms, household income < $210,000, credit checks apply; can cover inverter/solar installed with the battery. 100,000 rebates capped; rates decrease annually 2026–2030. Batteries installed before 1 July 2025 ineligible.
- Stacked federal + state on 10 kWh: up to ~$5,000 (Synergy area) / ~$7,500 (Horizon area).
- Sources: https://www.wa.gov.au/organisation/energy-policy-wa/wa-residential-battery-scheme ; https://www.plenti.com.au/wa-residential-battery-scheme ; https://www.synergy.net.au

### Synergy/SWIS technical rules (from 1 May 2026)
- New batteries must meet AS/NZS 4777.2:2020 + CSIP-AUS or accept a 1.5 kW export cap; aggregate inverter cap raised to 30 kVA; pre-May-2026 installs grandfathered.
- DEBS feed-in: 2.25c/kWh off-peak, 10c/kWh peak (3–9pm); retail power ~28–35c/kWh. Synergy Battery Rewards VPP pays 70c/kWh during activation events (≤30/yr).

- Grid-tied battery Perth: 10–13 kWh $12k–$16k before rebates (~$7.6k after stacking in Synergy area; ~$5.1k Horizon area). Per-kWh installed ~$664–$1,490 (smaller = dearer per kWh).

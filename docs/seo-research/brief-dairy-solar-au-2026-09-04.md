## 2. Competitive landscape (US)

- Farm Solar Guide "solar milk cooling" (~2,900 words; vendor-style blog with AI-slop tells — figures treated UNVERIFIED): frames milk cooling as 30-40% of dairy electricity; PMO-style 38F/2-hour rule as the hard constraint; strategies: DC compressor direct-drive, ice-bank thermal storage, hybrid; composite case 8 kW PV + 40 kWh LiFePO4, $34k gross / $23.8k after 30% ITC, 5.4-yr payback; 40 kWh = 12-16 h compressor; insulated 3,000-gal tank loses 1-2F/day.
- eXtension Farm Energy / UW-Madison (peer-reviewed): milk enters at 98F, must reach ~38F (56 Btu/lb); well-water precooler cuts milk-cooling cost up to 60%; RHR and precooler compete for the same heat — audit before both; scroll compressors 15-20% more efficient than reciprocating.
- UMaine disaster guide (~1,800 words): outage = vacuum pump stops (cannot milk), bulk tank loses active cooling, warm milk added during outage accelerates spoilage; prescribes separate gensets for parlor vs feed/water, load-testing, hauler coordination pre-storm; never ship outage-affected milk without sign-off.
- Northeast Farm Energy IQ (Penn State/Rutgers/UVM): sizing workflow — 12 months of bills, kW demand + demand charges, efficiency FIRST then PVWatts; key line: annual-offset PV does not remove grid dependence for concentrated milking/cooling loads.
- Iowa State (PM 2089X): VSD vacuum pump roughly halves energy (18,396 kWh/yr example).
- USDA REAP: grants $2,500-$1M up to 50% federal share; PAUSED for grants as of 2026-03-31 FAQ; stand-alone batteries ineligible, batteries only paired with renewables. (US-only; AU readers need AU scheme mapping instead.)

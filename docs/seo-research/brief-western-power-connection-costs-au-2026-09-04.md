The page decodes fees, flags traps (design-fee invoice before quote, private pole replacement at own cost, underground-after-demolition), explains WP vs Horizon split and the obligation-to-connect map, then hands to the off-grid decision guide (which already ranks for "off grid vs grid connection wa").

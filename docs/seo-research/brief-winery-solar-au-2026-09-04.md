## 2. Competitive landscape (US)

- Korbel / EnergyToolBase case study (~400 words): 852 kW PV + 360 kW/798 kWh BESS; driver was demand charges (up to $22/kW) and a 980 kW export cap, not resilience; 90% average bill offset.
- Domaine Carneros / Microgrid Knowledge (~1,200 words): 427 kW solar + 250 kVA battery + generator; 70-80% of load supplied; motivation = earthquake/fire/PSPS resilience; crush-critical loads: "lose power at harvest and fruit overripenes; lose cellar temperature control and wine quality suffers"; ~10-yr ROI model; six months of controls fine-tuning.
- UC Davis Brewery-Winery microgrid guide (~1,800 words): 120 kW PV + ~280 kWh second-life EV battery, DC-coupled, explicitly NOT islandable; cites LBNL: California winemaking uses over 400 GWh/yr, second-largest electricity-consuming food industry in the state; refrigeration is the dominant load.
- CEC final report (abstract): 200 kW PV + 280 kWh storage across two winery buildings: up to 39% peak reduction, ~$12,000/yr savings, ~14,000 kg CO2/yr.
- SolarCraft / Duckhorn (~350 words): 400 kW DC, ~72% offset, no battery, no costs, no payback.
- Vitivoltaics (Sun'Agri/INRAE, France, 6-year trial): shading delays veraison 2-5 days and harvest up to 7 days, acidity retained at similar sugar, wines described as "fruitier"; framed as climate adaptation, not an energy product.

Common sections (must-cover): case-study numbers, refrigeration as the dominant load, resilience motivation story, demand-charge/rate-structure economics.
Content gaps (all US pieces miss): no buyer's guide exists; no sizing methodology (US pieces are vendor case studies or academic); no off-grid framing (all grid-tie); no crush-season load math; no costs on most pages; nothing AU.
Differentiation: the AU page is the buyer's guide the US SERP lacks: sizing method, honest architecture choice (microgrid vs full off-grid vs grid-tie), WA region reality, and RENOZ's published platform numbers.

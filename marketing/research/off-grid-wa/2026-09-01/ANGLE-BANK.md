# Evidence-backed angle bank

Research directory: `marketing/research/off-grid-wa/2026-09-01/`

## Ready for production

### 1. Start with the loads

- Priority: high
- Buyer question: What size off-grid system does my property actually need?
- Hook: `What size off-grid system does your property actually need? Start with the loads.`
- Funnel job: move a problem-aware buyer into structured project-fit research
- Offer and CTA: a plain-language sizing guide; `Learn More`
- Proof: RENOZ serves rural properties, pumps, and workshops; no capacity recommendation is made before the property inputs are known
- Destination: `https://renoz.energy/guides/battery-sizing-off-grid-wa`
- Supporting evidence: S02, S03, R02, R03, C01, P01
- Claim boundary: do not promise autonomy, reliability, savings, or a system size
- Exclusions: caravan, portable, DIY-only, and grid-only battery enquiries
- Status: ready

### 2. Plan the whole power system

- Priority: high
- Buyer question: How do solar, batteries, peak loads, the inverter, and generator backup fit together?
- Hook: `Battery capacity is only one part of an off-grid power system.`
- Funnel job: help a solution-aware buyer understand the architecture and qualify for a technical discussion
- Offer and CTA: generator-hybrid planning guide; `Learn More`
- Proof: RENOZ supports hybrid solar-battery-generator integration; the Harvey record includes solar, battery, inverter path, and retained generator backup
- Destination: `https://renoz.energy/guides/diesel-to-battery-wa-farms`
- Supporting evidence: S04, R04, C02, C04, P01, P02
- Claim boundary: no fuel-saving, run-time, reliability, or displacement promise
- Exclusions: generator-only sales and portable generator enquiries
- Status: ready

### 3. A real WA system record

- Priority: medium
- Buyer question: What does an installed WA off-grid system actually include?
- Hook: `Harvey, WA: the published system record.`
- Funnel job: give comparison-stage buyers concrete first-party evidence without a superiority claim
- Offer and CTA: Harvey case study; `View Case Study`
- Proof: 35.8 kWh gross RENOZ LV stack, 21 kWp solar, Selectronic SPMC482 plus Fronius Primo, and generator backup retained
- Destination: `https://renoz.energy/case-studies/harvey-farm`
- Supporting evidence: S05, C01, P02
- Claim boundary: state only the published system facts; do not claim savings, reliability, customer satisfaction, or generalise performance
- Exclusions: do not describe AI-assisted canvas extensions as the original installation photograph
- Status: ready

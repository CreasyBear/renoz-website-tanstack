# Research pack: is-it-worth-going-off-grid-wa (WORTHIT)

Date: 2026-09-02. Every guide claim traces to an entry below. Community figures attributed as community-posted; no fixed rebate totals published.

## Verified sources

1. **Clean Energy Regulator — Solar batteries (off-grid eligibility)**
   https://cer.gov.au/schemes/renewable-energy-target/small-scale-renewable-energy-scheme/small-scale-renewable-energy-systems/solar-batteries
   Established: off-grid battery eligibility hinges on being at least 1 km from the nearest main-grid line, OR (if closer) written evidence that total grid connection cost exceeds $30,000 (exactly $30,000 is not enough; connection-point-to-building costs excluded). Dwelling must be lived-in; 5–100 kWh nominal; CEC-approved battery; SAA-accredited installer. Supports: CER rule paragraph; break-even trigger table.

2. **WA Government — Household electricity pricing (Energy Policy WA)**
   https://www.wa.gov.au/organisation/energy-policy-wa/household-electricity-pricing
   Established: Synergy residential A1 from 1 July 2025: daily supply charge $1.1605/day incl GST; usage 32.3719 c/kWh. Horizon A2 residential matches ($1.1605/day; 32.3719 c/kWh) under the Uniform Tariff Policy. Supports: annual grid-cost arithmetic (supply alone ≈ $424/yr; 15 kWh/day usage ≈ $1,773/yr; ≈ $2,200/yr total).

3. **Horizon Power — 2025 fees and charges**
   https://solar.horizonpower.com.au/about-us/news-announcements/fees-and-charges-2025/
   Established: A2 residential first-dwelling supply $1.1605/day from 1 July 2025; Uniform Tariff Policy statement. Supports: regional tariff parity claim.

4. **Western Power — Single residential connection (2025-26 fees)**
   https://www.westernpower.com.au/products-services/install-something-new/connect-my-home-or-business/new-residential-connections/single-residential-connection/
   Established: application $506; design $1,353 / $3,388 / $6,776 by complexity; construction quoted separately after design. Notes WPS may offer a stand-alone power system where the network is scheduled for decommissioning. Supports: connection-fee paragraph; SPS mention.

5. **Reddit r/AusFinance — "Should I go off grid for power (advice needed)"**
   https://www.reddit.com/r/AusFinance/comments/14yg8mf
   Established (community-posted, attributed as such): SA homeowner quoted $22,000 for ~100 m underground extension plus civil works, total could exceed $40,000; commenter view that at $20–40k batteries may be better value. Supports: the "$40k+ makes off-grid genuinely competitive" community threshold, attributed as community framing, not RENOZ claim.

6. **NSW Government (energy.nsw.gov.au) — Home solar battery off-grid guide (PDF)**
   https://www.energy.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/2022-08/2020_02_NSW_HomeSolarBattery_OffGrid.pdf
   Established: a true off-grid system needs more solar, larger batteries and usually a generator; indicative $25,000–$75,000 upfront for an average NSW off-grid hybrid system; designing a grid-connected system to cover 80–90% of demand is generally more cost-effective than full off-grid. Supports: "when staying connected wins" section; sceptic perspective.

7. **Solar Victoria — Grid vs grid connection**
   https://www.solar.vic.gov.au/grid-vs-grid-connection
   Established: off-grid system sized for 10 kWh/day could cost about $55,000 installed. Supports: sceptic cost perspective (second source for off-grid cost realism).

8. **Australian Government — energy.gov.au, how solar pays itself / batteries reduce bills**
   https://www.energy.gov.au/solar/financial-benefits-solar/how-solar-pays-itself-and-batteries-reduce-bills
   Established: worked example $22,000 solar+battery saving $2,100/yr ≈ 10.5-year simple payback vs ~10-year battery warranty; government caution that a battery may not pay for itself within its lifetime. Supports: payback honesty paragraph; 10-year warranty horizon framing.

9. **DCCEEW — Cheaper Home Batteries Program**
   https://www.dcceew.gov.au/energy/programs/cheaper-home-batteries
   Established (shared claim boundary): CHBP available to off-grid systems; no grid-connection condition; no VPP condition; roughly 30% off at point of sale. Supports: rebate wording, identical every appearance.

10. **Internal — battery-sizing-off-grid-wa.ts and off-grid-system-cost-wa.ts**
    Established: installed ranges $15k–$30k (weekender), $40k–$65k (3-bed), $70k–$120k+ (farm); batteries 40–50% of system cost; DoD divisor 0.8 for LiFePO4. Supports: cost ranges (cross-checked consistent between both guides).

11. **Internal — HARVEY_SCORECARD (guide-types.ts) + /case-studies/harvey-farm**
    Established: Harvey WA farm, 35.8 kWh gross usable scorecard, 21 kWp solar, Selectronic SPMC482 + Fronius Primo, $200,000 grid quote avoided. Supports: the worked remote example.
